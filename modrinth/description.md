**Build tiny worlds you can step inside.** Place a snow globe, sneak and right-click it, and you're standing inside, in a build space of its own. Step back out and everything you made is there in miniature under the glass: the blocks, the water, and the mobs living in it, moving live.

## Eight kinds of globe

Snow globe, terrarium, storm globe, ship in a bottle, desert globe, aquarium, star globe and nether jar. Each has its own weather and sky, inside and out: snow that turns with the seasons, lightning and thunder in the storm globe, blown sand at sunset in the desert, the northern lights over the star globe, embers rising in the nether jar.

## From inside, the world outside is giant

Look out through the glass and the room the globe stands in is there, a hundred times bigger: the shelf, the globes next to it, and the players and animals walking past. Shake the globe from outside and everyone inside feels it. Knock on the glass and they hear a giant knock.

## Things to do with them

- **Shake** a snow globe to turn the year: winter, spring, summer, autumn. Shaken into winter, real snow settles inside.
- **Fish** go in from their bucket, and a water bucket catches one back. Animals on a lead come in with you.
- **Globes inside globes**: put one in another and go in deeper still.
- **Twin globes**: two globes, one world inside. Keep one at home and give one to a friend.
- **Display them** on a collector's shelf or a spinning turntable, and name them with a name tag for a brass plate on the base.
- **Create schematics** can be built inside a globe (Create is optional). Globes grow to fit, up to 256 blocks across.

## Getting globes

Every globe starts as a glass bulb, blown from glass with a glassblower's pipe. Your first globe comes with the *Globe Keeper's Handbook*, with all the recipes. Wandering traders and old chests sometimes have a globe with a little world already in it, and there's an advancement trail to follow.

## Survival-friendly

Nothing comes from nowhere: a globe made in survival starts empty, a schematic is paid for block by block, and a globe never shrinks if that would cut blocks off.

## Notes

- Install on the server **and** every client.
- Settings (lightning, weather inside, tiny thunder outside) are in *Mods → Worlds in Glass → Config*.
- Made for NeoForge 1.21.1. Played in single player and on LAN; it starts and runs its tests on a dedicated server, but has seen less play there.
